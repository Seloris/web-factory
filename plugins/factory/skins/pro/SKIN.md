# pro

Dense and neutral, for screens people work in all day. 32px controls, rows as ruled lines instead of cards, a small title, a calm blue accent, numbers aligned.

**Fonts:** IBM Plex Sans (400–600); fallback `system-ui, sans-serif`. Tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) wherever figures line up.

**Do**
- Compact spacing (8px rows, 12–20px between groups); several controls per line.
- Rows ruled (a hairline top and bottom), no gap; small square-ish badges.
- Status shown with a badge or a colored label, not with large blocks of color.

**Avoid**
- Large titles, big empty areas, heavy shadows, decorative imagery.
