# soft

Rounded and friendly. Pastel green page, white rounded surfaces, large touch targets (48px controls), butter-yellow tags, a bold rounded title.

**Fonts:** Nunito (400, 600, 700, 800); fallback `ui-rounded, system-ui, sans-serif`.

**Do**
- Large radii (`--radius` 26px, rows 18px), roomy padding, controls at `--control-h`.
- Rows as cards with a soft bottom edge (`--item-shadow`), separated by a gap.
- Short, warm copy; empty states that say what to do next.

**Avoid**
- Sharp corners, dense layouts, small hit areas, thin type weights.
