# swiss

Black, white and one red. A very large, tightly tracked title, uppercase spaced labels, a strict grid, no cards and no shadows. The typography does the work.

**Fonts:** Schibsted Grotesk (400, 500, 700, 800); fallback `'Helvetica Neue', Arial, sans-serif`.

**Do**
- No panel: content on the page, aligned on one left edge; lists ruled (a 2px top rule in `--text`, a hairline under each row).
- The title as large as the layout allows, weight 800, negative tracking.
- Red only for the primary action, tags (as red uppercase words) and errors.

**Avoid**
- Rounded corners, shadows, tinted surfaces, centered layouts, a second accent color.
