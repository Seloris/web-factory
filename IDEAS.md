Grouped by scope: one `## <scope>` section per area (`factory`, `board`, a skill, a stack pack), a new scope's section added at the bottom; in a section, new items appended at the bottom, no priorities or metadata. Format: `- {YYYY-MM-DD} — **<Short title>:** <The idea in a sentence or two, as the person put it>.` An idea taken up leaves the list in the change that takes it up.
